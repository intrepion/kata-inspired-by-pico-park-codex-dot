# Little Lights
Two creatures restore a garden together.

## Language
**Sol**: The golden creature who lights sun beacons.
**Luna**: The violet creature who lights moon beacons.
**Beacon**: A role-specific garden light that remains lit once activated.
_Avoid_: Pressure plate
**Gate**: A barrier opened by its linked beacons.
**Shared key**: A key retained by the team for the current stage.
_Avoid_: Inventory item
**Portal**: The exit that welcomes both creatures after the garden is restored.
**Stage**: One complete garden puzzle.

**Planter**: A wheeled garden box that friends can push along a rail and climb to reach a light.
_Avoid_: Crate
